Doctors: three design directions

A

Soft clinical

Light, airy and friendly. White cards with soft rounded corners, and the clinic's colour on the selected item, the main buttons and the patient now in the room. Plus Jakarta Sans, the same family as Dukaloo. Easy to read for long days at reception.

B

Crisp desk

Compact and professional, like a hospital system done well. A dark sidebar, thin lines, square corners, and lists that show more patients without scrolling. The clinic's colour is used sparingly, as a marker line. IBM Plex Sans. Best for busy clinics with several doctors.

C

Warm calm

Soft and reassuring. Warm paper-like surfaces instead of cold grey, round buttons and turn numbers, larger text, and a floating tab bar on phones. The clinic's colour fills the main figure. Nunito, a rounded font. Feels more like a family practice than a hospital.

Everything above is placeholder data. Whichever direction you choose, the app uses only colour tokens, works in light and dark, mirrors fully for Kurdish and Arabic, and takes the clinic's own colour from Ariloo.
